◆ Our Story

Woven from the
Heart of Morocco

High in the Atlas Mountains, where the air is thin and the light turns gold at dusk, Berber women have been spinning yarn for over a thousand years. Freewill was born to bring that living tradition to the world — unchanged, uncompromised.

We work directly with small cooperatives of artisan women in the Azilal and Ourika valleys. Every skein is hand-spun from local organic wool, then dyed using plants, minerals, and roots found in the mountain landscape: saffron, indigo, henna, walnut shell, pomegranate skin.

No chemicals. No shortcuts. Just fibre, water, fire, and skill passed down through generations.

— The Freewill Family

◆ From Mountain to You

How Every Skein is Made

A slow, careful process that cannot be rushed

1

Shearing

Local sheep are sheared once a year in spring using traditional hand tools on mountain farms.

2

Washing & Carding

Fleece is washed in mountain spring water and carded by hand to align the fibres.

3

Natural Dyeing

Skeins are simmered in plant-based dye baths — saffron, indigo, henna, walnut — for rich, lasting colour.

4

Hand Spinning

Artisan women spin each skein on a traditional drop spindle, giving it its signature character and feel.
